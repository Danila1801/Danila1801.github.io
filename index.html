How I work

The model is rarely the hard part. The hard part starts once the endpoint is public: who pays when someone abuses it, what it says when it doesn’t know, and how you tell whether your last change actually helped or just changed something.

So I tend to write the rate limiter before the landing page, and the “I don’t know” path before the happy one.
